# Farmer prototype feature map

- Dashboard: active slot, token, queue, payment shortcuts.
- Booking: grain/perishable, custom crop names, 7-day slots, capacity and past-time protection.
- Perishable: express 04:00–08:00 window.
- Digital pass: generated QR + gate verification flow.
- Geo-fence: 2 km prototype verification against mandi coordinates.
- Help: keypad/IVR, toll-free and voice journey documentation.
- Assistant: predefined farmer help options.
- Header: clickable notifications and profile/settings panel.
- Languages: English, Hindi, Punjabi and Tamil selector remains available.

The prototype keeps an in-memory mock API. Production telecom, Bhashini, GPS, QR scanning and database integrations are intentionally deferred.
